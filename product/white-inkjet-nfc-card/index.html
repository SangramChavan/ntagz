About the Inkjet Printable NFC Card

If you own — or plan to buy — a PVC-tray inkjet printer such as the Epson L8050/L805, this card is built for it. The surface has an inkjet-receptive coating that absorbs ink cleanly for photo-quality, full-colour prints you can do yourself in-house, without sending files to a print shop. The NTAG215 chip underneath works exactly like our standard black/white cards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which printers is this compatible with?

Any PVC-tray-capable inkjet printer, most commonly the Epson L8050 and L805 series used for ID card printing in India.

Will regular inkjet printers work?

No — you need a printer with a PVC card tray/feeder. Standard paper-only inkjets can't feed rigid PVC cards.

Does printing affect the NFC chip?

No, the chip and antenna are laminated inside the card body; only the outer coated surface receives ink.

Can I still program the chip after printing?

Yes, printing and NFC programming are independent — program it before or after printing using our free browser tool.
