About the NTAG216 Adhesive Tag

This is a self-adhesive NFC sticker built on the NTAG216 chip — nearly double the memory of the more common NTAG213/215 tags. It's built for scenarios where you need to store more data per tag: a longer tracking URL with parameters, a multi-record NDEF message, or structured asset metadata. The adhesive backing sticks to most flat, non-metal surfaces (packaging, plastic, glass, wood).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this stick permanently?

The adhesive is designed for long-term application; it's not intended to be repeatedly removed and reapplied.

Can it be read through packaging?

Yes, NFC reads through thin non-metal materials like cardboard, plastic, and glass — thickness affects range slightly.

What surfaces should I avoid?

Avoid sticking directly on metal — use our anti-metal tag variant for that instead.

How much data can I store?

Up to 888 bytes usable — enough for a URL with tracking parameters or a compact multi-field NDEF record.
