About the Mini NFC Tag

This is our smallest-format NFC tag, purpose-built for makers and jewellers who need to hide an NFC chip inside a finished piece rather than stick a visible tag on the surface. It's commonly embedded during 3D printing (paused mid-print to drop the tag in), set into resin jewellery, or fitted into custom smart rings — all while staying small enough to disappear into the object.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work if fully embedded (no visible tag)?

Yes, as long as it isn't embedded directly against metal — resin, plastic, wood, and most non-metal materials don't block the signal.

How close does a phone need to be to read it?

Typically 1–3cm given the smaller antenna size — slightly shorter range than our full-size cards.

Can I still program it after embedding?

Program the tag before final embedding for easiest access; NFC data can technically still be rewritten afterward as long as a phone can get close enough.

What sizes are available?

The standard mini tag measures 21×11mm; contact us on WhatsApp for custom sizing requirements for specific jewellery settings.
